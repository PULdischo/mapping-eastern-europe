**Book Project** by Ivan N. Petrov, University of Lodz (PI)  
**Overview** 
A research project financed by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education (National Program for the Development of Humanities, Universalia 2\.1\), and realized by scholars from the University of Lodz (Faculty of Philology: Ivan N. Petrov, Marek Majer). The aim of the project is to prepare a comprehensive English translation of the scientific monograph, authored by Ivan. N. Petrov *From Incunabula to First Grammars. Contexts of the Development of the Bulgarian Literary Language (Late 15th – Early 17th Century)*, originally published in Polish by the University of Lodz Press. English version of the monograph will be published by **[Lexington Books](http://www.LexingtonBooks.com)**, which is a member of **[Rowman \& Littlefield](http://www.rowman.com)** Publishing Group.
