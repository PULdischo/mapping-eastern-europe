<p><strong>Book Project </strong>by Ivan N. Petrov, University of Lodz (PI)<br />
<strong>Overview</strong><br />
A research project&nbsp;financed by the Ministry of Science and Higher Education (National Program for the Development of Humanities, Universalia 2.1), and realized by scholars from the University of Lodz (Faculty of Philology: Ivan N. Petrov, Marek Majer). The aim of the project is to prepare a comprehensive English translation of the scientific monograph, authored by Ivan. N. Petrov <em>From Incunabula to First Grammars. Contexts of the Development of the Bulgarian Literary Language (Late 15th &ndash; Early 17th Century)</em>, originally published in Polish by the University of Lodz Press. English version of the monograph will be published by <strong><a href="http://www.LexingtonBooks.com">Lexington Books</a></strong>, which is a member of <strong><a href="http://www.rowman.com">Rowman &amp; Littlefield</a></strong> Publishing Group.<br />
<b>Further Reading</b><br />

Lanham: Rowman &amp; Littlefield, forthcoming.</p>
